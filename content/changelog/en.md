What's new in MultivRSS, in plain language.

## v0.2.1 — August 2026

**New**
- Save and tag articles right from Reader Mode
- Copy article text or download it as a Markdown file from Reader Mode
- This changelog page, so you can see what's new without digging through release notes
- An indicator shows when there's a new blog post you haven't read yet

**Improved**
- Source names in your feed are now clickable, taking you straight to that source
- Back navigation is more reliable, including when MultivRSS is installed as an app on your phone
- Tidied up the layout of source and category pages

## v0.2.0 — August 2026

**New**
- FAQ page answering the questions people ask us before signing up
- Reader Mode: read the full article without leaving MultivRSS
- Save any link from anywhere on the web, not just your feeds, and tag it for later
- YouTube channels can now be added as feed sources
- A curated directory of suggested feeds to help you find your first sources
- Delete your account and all your data yourself from Settings, no need to email us
- Import / export a CSV file with your list of feeds or your list of saved links
- Progressive Web App: install MultivRSS on your phone's home screen and open it like an app, no browser required

**Improved**
- Search now covers your saved links too, not just your feeds
- Also installable from desktop, not just mobile

## v0.1.0 — Initial release

- The first version: feeds organized by category, read and unread, save and unsave, and automatic background sync.
