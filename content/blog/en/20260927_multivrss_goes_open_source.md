It was my plan from day one. It took longer than expected, but today MultivRSS is finally *open source*, under the MIT license: you're free to take it and do whatever you want with it. There's a short [self-hosting guide](https://github.com/trnqeu/multivrss/blob/main/SELF_HOSTING.md) on GitHub.

As I wrote in the README, I won't have as much time to maintain the project as I'd like, so I can't accept outside pull requests. Bug reports and ideas are still welcome as [Issues](https://github.com/trnqeu/multivrss/issues), and if you want to take MultivRSS in a different direction, that's what forks are for. MultivRSS started as a personal project, and it will stay one for the foreseeable future.
