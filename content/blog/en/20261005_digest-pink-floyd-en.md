I'm crazy about these short Guardian interviews with the great musicians of our time. A few weeks ago, to mark the release of the new Durutti Column record, they interviewed Vini Reilly, one of the musicians I love most, full stop:

::digest[vini-reilly]

This week, on the other hand, they ran a much shorter but much funnier interview with Elvis Costello, who had the courage to say something I've always kept to myself: I don't like Pink Floyd.

While I'm at it, let me remind you that the Guardian is a wonderful example of how RSS feeds should be handled on a website. For every section of the paper you just add /rss to find the feed, so in this case the section is [theguardian.com/music](https://www.theguardian.com/music) and the feed lives at [theguardian.com/music/rss](https://www.theguardian.com/music/rss). Beautiful.

::digest[costello-pink-floyd]

Changing the subject completely, another piece that made my jaw drop this week tells the incredible story of how artificial intelligence made it possible to track down a source documenting one of the rare sightings of a dodo.

If, like me, you keep deep in your heart the misplaced hope that one day we'll see flocks of dodos strutting around the planet again, you have to read this one.

::digest[dodo]

Then there's a piece I still don't quite know what to make of. Do I agree? Do I disagree? I'd probably like to disagree, but can't find a way to do so.

The title, fairly eloquent, is "Intellectuals are fucking idiots", and it's about how dangerous it is to think you can reduce reality to models of reality.

::digest[intellectuals]
