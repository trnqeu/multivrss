I recently started rewatching (for the third time? Fourth?) [Community](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1439629/), one of the funniest TV shows in recent television history. One of the show's quirks was trying to relaunch the career of [Chevy Chase](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000331/?ref_=fn_t_1), the legendary comic actor who came out of the Saturday Night Live school, hugely popular in the 1980s and then forgotten in the decades that followed.


I came across this long 2018 Washington Post article that profiles the life, personality and career of a difficult, controversial, brilliant and often detestable figure, surfacing some details of his life and childhood that help put his character in context.
